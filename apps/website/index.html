AllieMinate

AllieMinate is a free, open-source desktop and mobile app that connects all of your cloud storage accounts (Google Drive, OneDrive, Backblaze B2, IDrive e2, MEGA, pCloud) into one unified workspace, and lets your own devices — Mac, Windows PC, and Android phone — sync files and share directly with each other over your local network. One workspace for every cloud you own, and every device you carry — with nothing routed through anyone's server but the providers themselves.

Download View on GitHub

Why AllieMinate asks for Google Drive access

When you click "Add Google Account" inside AllieMinate and sign in, the app requests access to your Google Drive so it can display your existing files and folders, and let you upload, download, rename, move, and organize them from within AllieMinate's own interface — the same way you'd use Drive's own website, just alongside every other cloud account you connect. Your Drive files and credentials are never sent to or stored on any AllieMinate-operated server: everything happens directly between your device and Google's own servers. See the Privacy Policy for full detail.

What it does

Cloud Aggregation

Every cloud account you use, in one searchable workspace — browse, upload, organize, no separate apps.

Device Pairing

Pair your phone, Mac, or Windows PC directly over your local network. No relay server in the middle.

Sync Pairs

Two-way, backup-only, or download-only sync between any local folder and a cloud account or device.

Universal Clipboard

Copy on your desktop, paste on your phone or another desktop, automatically.

Nearby Share

Send a file to any AllieMinate device on the same network instantly, even before it's paired.

Open Source

The whole thing, not a trial. Read the code, build it yourself, or contribute.
